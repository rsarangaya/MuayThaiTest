Settings & controls

Arrows/WASD/QEZC move · Space wait · R rest · X scent · I pack · G pick up · L log · > / < stairs · TAB inspect · Shift+1-9 quick buttons.

Tap the map to travel, bump to attack, right-click or long-press a monster to inspect it. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap the minimap to jump.

Tap an attached graft in your pack to Detach, Devour, or use its active ability. Red warning tiles show where a blow lands next turn.

Descend 20 floors in four stages of five. Runs autosave as you play, so you can leave and come back, but there is no manual save or load: a bad move stays bad.
